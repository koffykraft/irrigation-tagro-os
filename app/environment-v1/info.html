Crop & application explorer
Use crop, spacing, water reality and your purpose to compare drip, inline, micro/mini sprinkler, jet, bubbler, mister/fogger or other suitable application approaches. Product names come after the application need.
Benefits & trade-offs
Show water/labour/runtime implications, crop-use suitability, convenience, expansion and capital cost separately. Monetary benefit must be labelled as an estimate with its evidence/source, never promised as guaranteed return.
Transparent comparison
TAGRO/Jain choices can be compared with generic or external alternatives. The design requirement remains visible so a user can understand what any alternative must satisfy.
From design to sale
A user may request a quote for all materials, only selected items, installation, or merely save the design. Buying is a continuation of the job, not a requirement for using the design tools.
