# TAGRO Irrigation OS — Budget Adviser Contract v0.1

Status: WORKING DESIGN

## 1. Purpose

Budget is a design constraint and decision aid, not a number the system should force a design to match.

## 2. Required behaviour

Given a budget, the Adviser may compare:

- complete current design
- staged installation
- priority-crop-first design
- future-ready backbone with deferred laterals/devices
- manual-operation alternative
- manufacturer-specific versus generic product alternatives
- reuse of verified existing infrastructure

## 3. Honesty rule

If the requested outcome does not realistically fit the stated budget, the system should say so clearly.

It must not hide omitted components, under-size the system, invent prices or force an unsafe/invalid design to meet the budget.

## 4. Trade-off explanation

Every budget-saving alternative should state what changes, for example:

- lower present cost but more manual work
- fewer irrigated zones now
- deferred secondary crop
- lower future expansion readiness
- generic product with less verified field/product evidence
- reuse conditional on inspection

## 5. Design continuity

A staged design should preserve the intended future relationship where possible, so later phases extend the accepted network rather than require a new disconnected design.

## 6. Governing sentence

**Treat budget as a real constraint, expose the consequences of compromise, and prefer honest staging over fictitious completeness.**
