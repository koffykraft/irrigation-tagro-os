# TAGRO Irrigation OS — Iteration 04: Reflective Capture

Status: WORKING / REVIEW
Starting junction: `iteration-02-page-structure` containing the signed Iteration 03 engine framework.

## Objective

Design the data-capture experience that feeds the signed irrigation engine while making input itself useful to the farmer as a thinking and analysis tool.

## Project blind

This junction does not change the signed logical engine.

It does not approve a final visual language, map provider, database, hydraulic engine, BOM, AI provider or production workflow.

It defines how consequential farm meaning should be captured and reflected before those replaceable implementations are attached.

## Working principles

1. Purpose and active task remain central.
2. Information flows inward as evidence and relationships rather than as isolated form fields.
3. An object is not fully understood by its name alone; role, relationship and purpose may materially change its meaning.
4. Multiple simultaneous purposes are allowed.
5. Farmer wording is preserved alongside normalized categories.
6. AI/system reflection is provisional until accepted or corrected.
7. Data capture should help the farmer understand the farm better.
8. Ask one useful thought at a time.
9. Prefer tap, choose, rank, speak, photo, map and draw over typing when sufficient.
10. Optional depth must feel optional, not like incomplete work.
11. SUB_MINIMUM remains the default depth.
12. One decision-changing question is better than many low-value fields.
13. The farmer can correct the system's interpretation at any point.
14. Relationships and purposes ripple only into dependent design elements.

## Example used to test the model

Banana may be a sole crop or an intercrop, and its purpose may include household food, supplemental income, weed suppression, cultural practice or support while another crop establishes.

The capture model preserves those distinctions and can reflect their likely design consequences without silently promoting inference to truth.
