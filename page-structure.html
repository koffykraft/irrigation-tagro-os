TAGRO OS · IRRIGATION · ITERATION 02 · PAGE STRUCTURE

Pages are surfaces, not silos.

This is the working page structure for the irrigation design system. The order and grouping are defaults only. Facts live in shared state; pages are planar projections over that state.

Governing rule

Move, hide, split, merge or replace a page without moving or duplicating the truth. Move a dock without changing its domain contract. Replace a provider without changing the farm.
